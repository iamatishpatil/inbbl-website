Privacy Policy

Friend Universe • INBBL (India Bubble) Platform

1. Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Friend Universe ("we", "us") collects when you use the INBBL (India Bubble) mobile app on Android and iOS (the "App") and our websites (the "Website"). Together these are the "Platform". "You" means anyone who visits or uses the Platform.

We are the Data Fiduciary for your personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules. This policy is our notice to you under those laws. It should be read with our Terms of Service. Where the two conflict on how we handle personal data, this policy wins.

You must be 18 or older to use the Platform. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account (see section 9).

Summary in plain words

  • We collect the information needed to operate your account and profile, let you create and share posts, stories and Sneaks (short videos), provide chat functionality and the Map feature when you grant location permission, and keep the Platform safe.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • INBBL is a free social app. We do not charge users for using the App and do not process in-app purchases, wallet balances or user-to-user money transactions.
  • Optional analytics, session replay, personalisation and marketing require separate consent where applicable. You can withdraw that consent at any time.
  • You can request account deletion through the App or by contacting us. Data is deleted or retained in line with the periods and legal requirements described in section 6.
  • Questions or complaints can be sent to our Grievance Officer (section 11).

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as described below:

  • Other users: Depending on your privacy settings and the nature of the feature, other users may be able to see your name, username, profile photo, profile information and content you make available through posts, stories, Sneaks and chats. Chat messages are visible to the users participating in the relevant conversation. Content shared publicly may be viewed, saved or reshared by others. You can use available privacy, block and report controls.
  • Service providers (Data Processors): We may use service providers for hosting, storage, content delivery, chat infrastructure, security, moderation, analytics, notifications and technical support. They may process personal data only to provide services to us and under appropriate contractual arrangements. The specific providers should be identified in this policy once the production vendor list is confirmed.
  • Legal and safety: We may disclose information when required by law, court order or lawful government request, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent fraud or abuse, or defend our rights.
  • Transfers outside India: If a service provider processes or stores personal data outside India, we will do so in accordance with applicable Indian data-protection requirements and contractual safeguards.

6. How long we keep it, deletion and security

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

Deleting your account: You may request account deletion through the App, if the option is available, or by contacting us using section 11. We will process the request in accordance with applicable law and the retention periods above. Some information may remain for a limited period in backups or where retention is legally required, needed to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or protect safety. Deleting your account cannot remove content that other users have already saved, copied or shared outside your account.

Where we store it: Personal data may be stored on our hosting and service-provider systems in India or other locations described in section 4, subject to applicable law.

Security: We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data, such as access controls, encryption where appropriate, secure media access and administrative safeguards. No system is completely secure. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will take the steps required by applicable law.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access information about the personal data we process and how we use it.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request erasure of personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in accordance with applicable law.
  • Raise a grievance with us and, where available under law, approach the relevant authority.

To exercise a right, contact the Grievance Officer using the details in section 11. We may need to verify your identity before responding.
